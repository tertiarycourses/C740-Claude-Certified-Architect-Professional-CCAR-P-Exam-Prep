# LEND-07 Mortgage Affordability

Access: all staff
Owner: Mortgage Lending · Effective: 1 August 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets how Northwind Bank assesses whether a customer can afford a home loan before an offer is made.

## 2. Total debt servicing ratio

A customer's total monthly debt repayments, including the new mortgage, must not exceed 55% of gross monthly income. This total debt servicing ratio (TDSR) is a hard limit and is not subject to credit exceptions.

## 3. Stress test

Affordability is calculated at the higher of the actual mortgage rate or a stress rate of 4% a year. An application that passes only at the actual rate is declined.

## 4. Tenure

The maximum mortgage tenure is 30 years, and the loan must end before the borrower's 65th birthday.

## 5. Valuation

Every mortgage needs an independent valuation from the bank's approved valuer panel. The loan amount is based on the lower of the valuation and the purchase price.
