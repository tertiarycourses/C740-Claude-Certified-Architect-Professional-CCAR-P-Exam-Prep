# LEND-04 Personal Loans

Access: all staff
Owner: Retail Lending · Effective: 1 October 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for unsecured personal loans offered to retail customers at any Northwind Bank branch or through the mobile app.

## 2. Eligibility

Applicants must be at least 21 years old and no older than 65 when the loan ends. They must have a minimum annual income of $30,000 and at least six months of salary credits or tax assessments as proof of income.

## 3. Loan limits

The maximum unsecured personal loan is six times the applicant's monthly income, capped at $50,000 across all of the customer's unsecured loans with Northwind. A request above the cap is a credit exception and is handled under LEND-11.

## 4. Tenure and pricing

Loan tenure is 12 to 60 months. The interest rate is set by the weekly pricing sheet; tellers quote the published rate and never negotiate it.

## 5. Early repayment

A customer may repay a personal loan early at any time. An early repayment fee of 1.5% of the outstanding principal applies if the loan is repaid within the first 24 months. After 24 months the fee is waived.
