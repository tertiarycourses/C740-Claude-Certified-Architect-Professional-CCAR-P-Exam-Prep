# LEND-11 Credit Exceptions

Access: credit_officer
Owner: Credit Risk · Effective: 1 September 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets who may approve a loan that falls outside a lending policy limit, and how the exception is recorded.

## 2. Approval authority

A senior credit officer may approve a personal loan up to 10% above the limit in LEND-04. Any exception more than 10% above the limit must be approved by the Credit Committee, which meets every Tuesday.

## 3. Conditions

An exception needs a written justification, at least 12 months of banking history with Northwind, and no missed repayment in the last 24 months.

## 4. Recording

Every approved exception is logged in the Credit Exception Register within one business day, with the approver's name and the justification.
