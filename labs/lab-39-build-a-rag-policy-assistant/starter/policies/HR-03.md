# HR-03 Leave

Access: all staff
Owner: Human Resources · Effective: 1 January 2026

## 1. Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 18 days of annual leave a year, pro-rated in the first year of service.

## 2. Carry forward

Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried forward into the next year, and must be used by 31 March. Exception: if a manager cancelled an employee's approved leave for business needs, up to 10 days may be carried forward, with the HR partner's approval.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive 14 days of paid outpatient sick leave a year. A medical certificate is needed for every day of sick leave.

## 4. Applying for leave

Leave is applied for in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance, except for sick leave and emergencies.
