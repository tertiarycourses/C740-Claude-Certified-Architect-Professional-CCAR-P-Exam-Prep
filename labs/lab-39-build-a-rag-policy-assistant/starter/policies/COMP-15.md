# COMP-15 Whistleblowing

Access: all staff
Owner: Group Compliance · Effective: 1 June 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how employees raise concerns about wrongdoing and how they are protected when they do.

## 2. How to report

Concerns can be raised with a line manager, by email to speakup@northwind.example, or anonymously through the Speak Up hotline at extension 7700.

## 3. Protection

No employee who raises a concern in good faith will be dismissed, demoted or disadvantaged because of it, even if the concern turns out to be unfounded.

## 4. Investigation

Group Compliance acknowledges every report within three business days and keeps the reporter's identity confidential.
