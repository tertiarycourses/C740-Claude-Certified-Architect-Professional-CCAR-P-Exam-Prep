# HR-14 Disciplinary Procedure

Access: hr_partner
Owner: Human Resources · Effective: 1 April 2026

## 1. Purpose

This procedure sets how the bank handles alleged misconduct fairly and consistently.

## 2. Investigation

The HR partner appoints an investigating manager from outside the employee's team within five business days of the allegation.

## 3. Suspension

An employee may be suspended on full pay for up to 14 days while serious misconduct is investigated. Suspension is not a disciplinary penalty.

## 4. Outcomes

Outcomes are no action, a verbal warning, a written warning, a final written warning or dismissal. The employee may appeal within 10 business days.
