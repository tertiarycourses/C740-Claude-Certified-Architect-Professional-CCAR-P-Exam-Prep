# COMP-12 Anti-Money-Laundering

Access: all staff
Owner: Financial Crime Compliance · Effective: 1 July 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets every employee's duties for preventing money laundering and terrorist financing.

## 2. Customer due diligence

Staff verify the identity of every new customer before an account is opened, using an original identity document. Accounts are never opened on a copy.

## 3. Cash transactions

Any cash deposit or withdrawal of $20,000 or more needs a signed source-of-funds declaration before it is processed.

## 4. Suspicious transactions

An employee who suspects money laundering files an internal suspicious transaction report to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) within one business day.

## 5. Tipping off

Staff must never tell a customer, or hint, that a suspicious transaction report has been or may be filed. Tipping off is a criminal offence.
