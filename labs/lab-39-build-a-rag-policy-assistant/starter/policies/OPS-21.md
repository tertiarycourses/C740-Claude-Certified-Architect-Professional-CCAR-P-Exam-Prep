# OPS-21 Incident Reporting

Access: all staff
Owner: Technology Risk · Effective: 1 May 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy makes sure IT and information security incidents are reported and handled quickly.

## 2. What to report

Report any suspected information security incident: a lost laptop or phone, a phishing email that was clicked, customer data sent to the wrong person, or a system outage affecting customers.

## 3. How and when to report

Report the incident to the IT Service Desk at extension 4000 within one hour of noticing it, even outside office hours. Do not try to investigate it yourself.

## 4. Severity

The Service Desk assigns a severity from 1 (critical) to 4 (low). Severity 1 incidents are escalated to the Chief Information Security Officer at once.
