# COMP-18 Gifts and Entertainment

Access: all staff
Owner: Group Compliance · Effective: 1 June 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy stops gifts and hospitality from influencing, or seeming to influence, business decisions.

## 2. Gifts from customers

Staff may accept a gift from a customer or supplier worth up to $150. Any gift above $150 must be declined, or handed to Group Compliance if it cannot be refused politely.

## 3. Cash

Cash and cash equivalents, such as vouchers or gift cards, must never be accepted, whatever their value.

## 4. Gifts register

Every gift accepted, whatever its value, is recorded in the Gifts Register within five business days.
