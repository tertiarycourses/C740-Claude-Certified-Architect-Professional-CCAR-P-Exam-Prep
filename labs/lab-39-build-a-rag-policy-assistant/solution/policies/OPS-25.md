# OPS-25 Cash Handling

Access: all staff
Owner: Branch Operations · Effective: 1 February 2026

## 1. Teller drawer limit

A teller drawer may hold up to $25,000 in cash. Cash above that limit is moved to the vault under dual control.

## 2. Dual control

The vault is opened only by two authorised staff together. Neither may hold both the key and the combination.

## 3. Till differences

At close of business each teller balances the till. A difference above $50, short or over, is reported to the branch manager the same day and logged in the Till Difference Register.
