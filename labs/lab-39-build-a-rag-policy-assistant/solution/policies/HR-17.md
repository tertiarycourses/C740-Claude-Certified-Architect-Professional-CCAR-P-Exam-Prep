# HR-17 Salary Bands

Access: hr_partner
Owner: Human Resources · Effective: 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the salary bands for branch and operations roles, and how pay is set within a band.

## 2. Branch bands

Teller: $2,800 to $3,600 a month. Senior teller: $3,400 to $4,300 a month. Branch manager: $7,500 to $9,800 a month.

## 3. Placement in band

New hires are placed in the lower half of the band unless the HR partner approves a higher starting salary.
