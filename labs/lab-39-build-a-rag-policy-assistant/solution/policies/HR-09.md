# HR-09 Remote Work

Access: all staff
Owner: Human Resources · Effective: 1 March 2026

## 1. Eligibility

Employees in office-based roles may work remotely up to two days a week with their manager's approval. Branch tellers and other roles that must be on site to serve customers are not eligible for remote work.

## 2. Working overseas

Remote work from outside the country is limited to 10 working days a year and needs the HR partner's approval in advance.

## 3. Equipment and security

Remote work uses only bank-issued laptops on the bank VPN. Customer data must never be printed at home.
